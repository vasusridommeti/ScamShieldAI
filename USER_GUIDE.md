# ScamShield AI: User Guide

## Opening the app
Double-click `index.html` in the `ScamShield-AI` folder. It opens in your browser and works without internet.

## Checking a message
1. Click **Analyze Message** in the left menu (on a phone, tap the menu button first).
2. Paste the message into the box. The counter shows how many of the 5,000 allowed characters you used.
3. Click **Analyze Message** (or press Ctrl + Enter).
4. Read the result:
   - **Risk meter and category:** Low, Moderate, High or Critical. This is an illustrative estimate, not a verdict.
   - **Your message, with suspicious phrases marked:** colours show how serious each phrase is.
   - **Warning signs found:** each card says what matched, why it matters and what to do.
   - **Recommended actions:** safe next steps.
5. Click **Analyze Another Message** to start again.

> A Low score or "no warning signs" does **not** mean a message is safe. If a message asks you to click, pay or share information, verify it through the official website, app or phone number.

## Sample messages
Under the text box, click a sample such as "Prize scam" to load it. Samples are illustrative, so they are not guarantees about real messages.

## Try a Demo
On the Dashboard, **Try a Demo** analyzes the first sample automatically. Use **Next demo sample** to step through all six.

## Scan History
- Every scan is saved on this device with its date, score and category.
- **View summary** shows the warning signs found.
- **Delete** removes one entry.
- **Clear history** removes everything (you will be asked to confirm).
- **Reset demo data** also clears settings such as language.
- The newest 100 scans are kept.

To store no message text at all, untick **Save a short preview in history** before analyzing.

## Privacy
Your message is analyzed inside your browser and never uploaded. Saved previews are masked (long numbers and e-mail addresses are hidden) and limited to 80 characters. Please do not paste real OTPs, passwords or card numbers.

## Language
Use the **English / తెలుగు** switch at the bottom of the menu. The Telugu text is an unreviewed draft and covers navigation, buttons, risk labels and tips.

## If you already lost money
Call your bank or payment provider immediately, then report at https://cybercrime.gov.in/ or call 1930 (confirm the number on the official portal).
