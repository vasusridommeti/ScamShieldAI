How a check works

  1. Paste. Copy the message text into the analyzer.
  2. Match. Fourteen fixed rules look for patterns such as OTP requests, upfront fees, threats and disguised links.
  3. Explain. You get a 0 to 100 indicator, the marked phrases and the reason behind each one.
  4. Decide. Use the recommended actions, then verify through an official channel.

The score is a heuristic, not a probability of fraud.

      Safety Tips

      Most scams rely on rushing you. These habits slow things down enough to stay safe.

      The Telugu text is an unreviewed draft. For important advice, please read the English version.

      OTP and password protection

      Your bank, UPI app or any genuine service will never ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV or password, whether by SMS, call or chat. Treat every such request as a scam, even if the sender already knows your name.

      Suspicious links

      Do not tap links in unexpected messages. Type the official website yourself or use the official app. Be wary of shortened links and addresses padded with words like secure, verify or kyc.

      Fake job offers

      Genuine employers do not charge for registration, training kits or security deposits. Check the company on its official website, and never pay to get a job.

      Advance-fee scams

      If you must pay a processing fee, tax or clearance charge before you receive a prize, loan or parcel, stop. Real prizes and loans do not work that way.

      Prize and lottery scams

      You cannot win a contest you never entered. Ignore messages that say you have won and ask you to click, pay or share details to claim it.

      Impersonation and urgency

      Scammers pose as banks, officials, couriers or support staff and rush you with threats like account blocked in 10 minutes. Pause, hang up, and call the number printed on your card or on the official website.

      Verifying payment requests

      Before sending money to a new account or UPI ID, confirm the request on a second channel you already trust, such as a known phone number or in person. Never approve an unexpected UPI collect request, and never scan a QR code to receive money.

      Reporting suspected fraud in India

      If money has already left your account, contact your bank or payment provider immediately and ask them to block the transaction or card. Then report the fraud.

      • Official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
      • National cyber crime helpline for financial fraud: 1930. Please confirm the number on the official portal before relying on it.
      • Keep screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers and links as evidence. Do not delete the message.

      About the Project

      The problem

      Phishing SMS, fake job offers, prize claims and payment-redirect messages reach people every day. They work because they create fear or excitement, and people rarely have a quick way to ask "what exactly is suspicious here?".

      The solution

      ScamShield AI is a scam-awareness assistant. It does not just say "scam" or "not scam". It marks the phrases that matched, explains why each pattern matters in plain language, and suggests safe next steps, so people learn to spot the signs themselves.

      Technology

      HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript, with the browser's localStorage for history. No framework, no build step, no external service. An optional Java Spring Boot backend in the optional-backend folder exposes the same rules as a REST API for future integrations.

      How detection works

      A rule-based engine checks fourteen explainable rules across ten categories: credential requests, upfront payments, urgent threats, unrealistic rewards, suspicious links, app-install requests, impersonation, money-transfer requests, job offers that require payment, and requests for sensitive information. Each rule counts once and adds a configurable weight. The weights are added up and capped at 100, then mapped to Low (0 to 24), Moderate (25 to 49), High (50 to 74) or Critical (75 and above).

      Sentences that give security advice, such as "never share your OTP", are not treated as requests.

      Privacy design

      Analysis happens in your browser and no message is sent anywhere. The page's Content Security Policy blocks network connections. History stores only a masked preview of up to 80 characters (or nothing, if you untick the option), the score and the rule IDs.

      Limitations

      • It is a fixed set of rules, not a trained AI model, and it cannot learn new scams by itself.
      • A score of 0 does not mean a message is safe. Careful scammers avoid known keywords.
      • Genuine messages can contain suspicious words and may score higher than expected.
      • Rules are tuned for English and common Indian scam patterns. The Telugu interface covers navigation, buttons, risk labels and safety tips, and the translation has not been reviewed by a native speaker.

      Future improvements

      • Optional language-model explanations behind the backend API, with explicit user consent.
      • More languages, reviewed by native speakers.
      • Link reputation checks against an official allow and block list.
      • A browser extension and an SMS share-sheet integration.

      ScamShield AI is a hackathon project built to teach scam awareness.

      Results are estimates from fixed rules. They cannot guarantee whether a message is genuine or fraudulent. When in doubt, contact the organisation through its official website or app.
